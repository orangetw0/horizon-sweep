Horizon Sweep

Trace your local skyline with the phone camera and export it as a .hrz file for N.I.N.A.

  1. Stand where the telescope stands.
  2. Put the crosshair on the top of the trees or roofline.
  3. Tap the red button once, then turn a full circle, tilting the phone so the crosshair follows the skyline.
  4. Export.

Before starting, wave the phone in a figure of eight to settle the compass, and keep away from cars and steel.
